# --- AI Search Bots ---
# GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and
# anthropic-ai intentionally have NO group of their own.
#
# Under RFC 9309 2.2.1 a crawler obeys exactly ONE group - the most specific
# one matching its token - and never inherits "User-agent: *". A group such as
#     User-agent: GPTBot
#     Allow: /
# is therefore that crawler's COMPLETE ruleset, and exempts it from every
# facet / crawl-trap rule below. Position in the file is irrelevant.
#
# Letting these tokens fall through to "User-agent: *" is what gives them
# access to real content AND subjects them to the crawl-trap blocks.
# Do not re-add per-bot groups without copying the full ruleset into them.
